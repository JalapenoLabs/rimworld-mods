# Changelog

2026.7.22 - Initial release. Adds the Break Loyalty ceremony, an Ideology ritual that can clear a prisoner's unwavering loyalty so they become recruitable. Success is the ritual quality (capped at 25%), raised by the speaker's social skill, spectators, faction disillusionment, shared ideoligion, colonist bonds, and prisoner contentment. Colony-wide 5-day cooldown; failure stacks a mood penalty that a success clears.
