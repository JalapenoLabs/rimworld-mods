- `SmashPhil.VehicleFramework`

### Relevant SOS2 behaviour

As of 2.8 SOS2 has a tactical combat layer: ships dodge based on thrust-to-weight
ratio, pilot Intellectual skill raises dodge chance, and weapon accuracy scales with
range. Odyssey gravships can land in SOS2 space and be built or upgraded there.
Odyssey compatibility is not final.
